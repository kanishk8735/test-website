Overview

The terms “Us,” “We,” and “Our” refer to www.ambria.in, while “You” and “Your” refer to the users of this website.

This Privacy Policy explains the privacy practices followed by www.ambria.in. By publishing this policy, we demonstrate our commitment to maintaining transparency and protecting the privacy of our users.

Through this policy, we inform users about the type of information collected through this website, how that information may be used or shared, the choices available regarding the use of personal data, and the security measures taken to protect such information.

Collection, Use and Sharing of Information

We are the sole owners of the information collected through this website. We only collect or access information that you voluntarily provide through our website forms, telephone calls, emails, or other direct communication with us.

We do not sell or rent your personal information to any individual or organisation.

We use your information primarily to understand the reason you contacted us, respond to your enquiry, and provide the services requested by you.

We may share your information with an authorised service provider, event vendor, or venue representative only when it is necessary to assist you in planning your wedding or event, or to fulfil a specific service request made by you.

We may contact you by telephone, email, WhatsApp, or other communication channels to respond to your enquiry, share information about our services, inform you about new offerings, or notify you of changes to this Privacy Policy.

You may opt out of future promotional communications at any time by contacting us by telephone or sending an email to sales@ambria.in.

You may also contact us to:

  • Know what personal information we hold about you.
  • Request corrections or updates to your personal information.
  • Express concerns regarding the use of your information.
  • Request the deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Security

We take appropriate measures to protect the personal information shared with us. Information provided by users is treated as confidential and is not disclosed except where necessary to respond to an enquiry, deliver a requested service, or comply with a legal obligation.

When you submit an enquiry, an authorised service provider, event vendor, or venue representative associated with us may contact you regarding your requirements.

Offline and Internal Security Measures

Access to personal information is limited to authorised employees, such as members of our billing, customer service, sales, or event management teams, who require the information to perform their professional responsibilities.

The computers, servers, and other electronic systems used to store information are maintained in secure environments. We take appropriate electronic, administrative, and physical measures to safeguard the information collected through our website.

We may disclose your personal information only under the following circumstances:

  • When you provide us with your written consent.
  • When disclosure is required by law or a competent legal authority.
  • When disclosure is necessary to provide a service specifically requested by you.

While we take reasonable precautions to protect your information, no method of transmitting or storing information over the Internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We therefore recommend that users also take appropriate precautions to protect their personal information and devices.

Any security breach occurring beyond the reasonable control of our standard security procedures shall be at the user’s own risk and discretion, subject to applicable laws.

Website Content

At Ambria Pushpanjali, we aim to provide useful and updated information about weddings, events, hospitality, catering, décor, wedding trends, styles, and related services.

To provide a rich and engaging user experience, our website may include photographs, videos, articles, testimonials, and other content provided by service vendors, venue representatives, photographers, event partners, and users.

We expect contributors and service providers to obtain the necessary permissions or hold the appropriate rights to any content supplied to us for publication.

If you are a user, service provider, venue representative, copyright owner, or other concerned party and have an objection to any content displayed on our website, please contact us immediately. We will review your request and take appropriate action where required.

Links to Other Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, including websites operated by service vendors, venue owners, event partners, and other online resources.

We are not responsible for the content, security, availability, privacy policies, or practices of third-party websites. We encourage users to carefully review the privacy policies and terms of use of any external website they visit, as those websites may independently collect personally identifiable information.

Cookies and Website Analytics

Our website may use cookies and analytics technologies to improve website functionality, understand visitor behaviour, measure website performance, and enhance the overall user experience.

You may manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality or performance of some parts of the website.

Updates to This Privacy Policy

Ambria Pushpanjali reserves the right to amend, revise, or update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes made to this policy will be published on this page and will become effective from the date of publication.

We encourage users to review this page periodically to remain informed about how we collect, use, and protect personal information.
